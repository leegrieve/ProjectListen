### Accessing the Bot
Visit the live application at: **https://ai-discovery-bot-au2m09sv.devinapps.com/**

No registration or login required - simply start chatting!

## 💬 How to Use Project LISTEN

### 1. **Starting a Conversation**
When you first visit the application, you'll see:
- A welcome message from your Access Group consultant
- An info box explaining the bot's purpose
- Sample conversation starters

**Example opening messages:**
- *"Summer is absolutely madness while winter is dead"*
- *"Staff scheduling is a nightmare with holiday requests"*
- *"I need to increase booking revenue by 20%"*
- *"We're still using paper menus and handwritten orders"*

### 2. **The Conversation Flow**
The bot follows a natural, consultative approach:

#### **Discovery Phase**
- The bot will ask about your business type and challenges
- Share your specific pain points and operational difficulties
- Be honest about what's not working in your current setup

#### **Understanding Phase**
- The bot will ask probing questions to understand your needs better
- Provide details about your business size, seasonality, and processes
- The more context you provide, the better the recommendations

#### **Solution Design Phase**
- Based on your responses, the bot will suggest relevant Access Group products
- Each recommendation is tailored to your specific challenges
- Ask questions about the suggested solutions

#### **Implementation Planning Phase**
- Discuss next steps and implementation considerations
- Get information about how to connect with an Access Group account manager

### 3. **Understanding the Interface**

#### **Chat Area**
- **Your messages**: Appear on the right in red bubbles
- **Bot responses**: Appear on the left in white bubbles
- **Info box**: Blue box at the top with consultant introduction
- **Input field**: Type your messages at the bottom

#### **Sidebar (Right Panel)**
The sidebar shows your progress and discoveries:

**Flight Path Progress**
- Visual stages showing your journey progress
- 4 stages: Discovery → Understanding → Solution Design → Implementation Planning
- Current stage highlighted in red

**Discovered Pain Points**
- Business challenges identified from your conversation
- Displayed as badges (e.g., "Seasonal Fluctuations", "Staff Scheduling")
- Updates automatically as you chat

**Recommended Solutions**
- Access Group products suggested for your needs
- Specific to your pain points, not generic bundles
- Shows product names and brief descriptions

**Conversation Summary**
- Quick metrics: number of messages, pain points, solutions
- Note that summary can be shared with your account manager

## 🎯 Best Practices for Effective Conversations

### **Be Specific About Your Challenges**
Instead of: *"We have problems"*
Try: *"Our restaurant gets overwhelmed in summer but is quiet in winter, and we struggle with staff scheduling during busy periods"*

### **Provide Context About Your Business**
- Industry (restaurant, retail, hospitality, etc.)
- Business size (number of locations, employees)
- Current systems and processes
- Seasonal patterns or special circumstances

### **Ask Follow-up Questions**
- *"How would this product help with my specific situation?"*
- *"What would implementation look like for a business like mine?"*
- *"Can you tell me more about how this addresses seasonal fluctuations?"*

### **Share Real Pain Points**
The bot is designed to help, not judge. Be honest about:
- Manual processes that waste time
- Technology gaps in your operations
- Staff management challenges
- Revenue or efficiency concerns

## 🔍 Understanding the Bot's Responses

### **Empathetic Acknowledgment**
The bot will often start responses by acknowledging your challenges:
- *"I completely understand that challenge"*
- *"That's a common pain point I see with seasonal businesses"*
- *"Summer can definitely be madness for hospitality operations"*

### **Probing Questions**
Expect follow-up questions to better understand your needs:
- *"How many covers do you typically serve during peak times?"*
- *"What's your current process for handling booking conflicts?"*
- *"Are you finding that manual processes are slowing down your operations?"*

### **Solution Explanations**
When recommending products, the bot will explain:
- Why this product fits your specific needs
- How it addresses your pain points
- What outcomes you might expect

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### **Bot Not Responding**
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again
- Ensure you're typing in the input field at the bottom

### **Recommendations Seem Generic**
- Provide more specific details about your business and challenges
- Ask follow-up questions about how solutions apply to your situation
- Share more context about your industry and operations

### **Technical Issues**
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Try a different browser or device
